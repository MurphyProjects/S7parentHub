Know Before You Go
First-year AA parent survival guide · U14 AA 2026-27
💰 Registration & Fees
Registration fees and payment schedule are communicated by the team manager. Budget separately for tournament hotels, meals, and gas on top of registration — overnight tournaments add up quickly. Your manager is the right contact for all financial questions.

📍 Where You'll Spend Your Saturdays
The northwest corridor is your second home. Kinburn CC (~23 visits last season), Stewart CC Pakenham (~11), and Carp Arena (~8) make up the majority. All are within 20–40 min of Stittsville. Pack a camp chair — not all rinks have comfortable spectator areas. Bell Sensplex (6 visits) is the exception — world-class facility.
🏨 Tournament Planning
Expect 4–5 tournaments per season, typically Thu–Sun. Budget $300–600 per tournament for hotel, food, and gas. Richmond Hill (Sept 18–20) is the first — book early, ~4.5 hrs from Ottawa. The team usually coordinates hotel blocks through the manager. Watch for a Northern Ontario trip (~4.5 hrs) in late November.
🇺🇸 Canton, NY — US Border Games (Oct 3 & Jan 30)
Silver Seven plays two regular season games across the border at Canton Ice Arena (Pavilion), Canton NY — Oct 3 and Jan 30. There are specific requirements every family should know before making these trips.

Documents required for all passengers:
Valid passport for every person in the vehicle. If you are driving a child who is not your own, you need a notarized parental consent letter signed by both parents of that child. The official interactive form is at travel.gc.ca/letter — it is free, Government of Canada issued, and recognized at the border.

Emergency medical insurance — critical gap:
As of January 1, 2020, OHIP provides zero coverage in the USA. Hockey Canada insurance is secondary only and requires you to have primary out-of-country coverage first — without it, Hockey Canada may not cover anything at all.

Check your group benefit plan before Oct 3: Most Ontario teacher and administrator group plans (OTIP/Manulife, Canada Life) include out-of-country emergency medical for dependants for trips up to 60–90 days. Call the number on your benefits card and ask specifically: "Does my plan cover my dependent child for emergency medical treatment in the USA resulting from a hockey injury?" If yes, carry that card and the emergency assistance number in the car. If no, purchase a single-trip travel insurance policy — ask specifically if it covers contact sports injuries.

Nearest emergency facilities to Canton Ice Arena:
— Urgent Care Canton · 80 E Main St, Canton NY · 9 AM–6 PM daily · Minor injuries (sprains, cuts, non-displaced fractures)
— Canton-Potsdam Hospital ER · 50 Leroy St, Potsdam NY · 315-265-3300 · 24/7 · Level III Trauma Center · ~15 min from arena
⚠ Head Injuries — Do Not Transport Across the Border
Most injuries (broken wrist, shoulder separation, cuts) can be stabilized at the rink or Urgent Care and treated back in Canada. Head injuries are the exception.

A serious concussion with loss of consciousness, worsening neurological signs, or suspected intracranial bleeding requires immediate CT imaging and possibly neurosurgical intervention. This cannot wait for a 90-minute drive and a border crossing.

Canton-Potsdam Hospital is a verified Level III Trauma Center — the only one in St. Lawrence County. It is specifically equipped for traumatic brain injury, has a Critical Care Unit, and is a Certified Primary Stroke Center. It works with Level I centres in Burlington and Syracuse for transfers when needed. No one is turned away for lack of insurance.

If a player loses consciousness, has a seizure, or shows worsening symptoms after a head impact — call 911 immediately. Do not drive to Canada.

Save this number in your phone now: Canton-Potsdam Hospital ER · 315-265-3300
🏒 U14 AA vs Rep B — What Changes
Body checking is now fully in effect. The game is faster, more physical, and more demanding than lower levels. Practice ice is more structured — less scrimmage, more systems. Kids will be tired. Sleep and nutrition matter more now. Don't be alarmed if the first few weeks are an adjustment — every player goes through it.

Helmet Safety
Virginia Tech Helmet Lab · Independent ratings · Updated 2025
⚠ U14 AA — Body checking is now fully in play
This is the first level where full contact is legal. Head impact risk increases meaningfully. The helmet your child wore at Rep B may be adequate — or it may not be. Worth checking before the season starts.
About the Virginia Tech Ratings
Virginia Tech's Helmet Lab independently tests hockey helmets using the STAR evaluation system — 48 lab impact tests per helmet simulating real on-ice impacts at low, medium, and high severity. They measure linear and rotational head acceleration correlated to concussion risk.

Lower score = better protection. More stars = better. This is the most credible independent helmet rating available — completely free from manufacturer influence.

Important Helmet price does not predict safety score. Some expensive helmets rate poorly. Some mid-range helmets are among the best.
Which ratings apply at U14?
VT publishes two separate hockey helmet rating systems:

Youth Hockey Ratings (2025 — new protocol) — tested with facemasks attached, impact conditions designed for youth hockey. Brands: Bauer, CCM, Warrior, TRUE, Valor, PowerTek.

Adult Hockey Ratings — for full-size adult helmets. Brands: Bauer, CCM, Easton, Mission, Reebok, Tour, True, Warrior.

At U14 players may be wearing either size. Check which your child wears and use the matching page.

How to read the ratings
Stars (1–5): More stars = better. Look for 4 or 5 stars.
Score: Lower = better protection. Counterintuitive but important.
Cost shown: Price at time of testing — may differ today.

Tip Filter by brand, find your child's current helmet, then compare against top-rated alternatives at the same price. A one-star difference at the same price point is always worth the swap at this level.
Beyond the rating — fit, age, cage
Fit matters as much as rating. A 5-star helmet that fits poorly is less protective than a 3-star helmet that fits perfectly. Have it professionally fitted at a pro shop.

Replace aging helmets. Foam compresses, plastic becomes brittle. Hockey Canada recommends replacing every 3–5 years or after any significant impact.

Full wire cage is mandatory at U14 in HEO. No visors. No exceptions.
If your child takes a significant hit
Follow Hockey Canada's Return to Play (RTP) protocol — a stepped progression back to full contact that should never be rushed. Your team's trainer will know this process. Full protocol at hockeycanada.ca. When in doubt, sit them out.
